# Tavern Zero — session-0002

Mode: separate agent requests with software dice. All characters remain together. Private intentions and discoveries are excluded from this table-facing transcript.

## scene-0001

Morning opens outside the Crooked Lantern, with all three travelers together at its doorway. The rain has eased to a fine drizzle. A muddy lane runs between low buildings toward a stone bridge visible a short walk away; water can be heard beneath its arch.

Beside the tavern entrance, a person in a patched gray coat presses a fresh notice onto a wooden board. Its large lettering reads: “NORTH SLUICE REPAIRS — ENQUIRIES AT THE BRIDGE.” The person steps back to check that the paper holds, then notices the travelers. “Morning.”

The familiar heading is plainly visible. Whether it leads to anyone who knows Dain remains unanswered.

### Turn 1 — Arlen Vale

> Morning. Who should we ask for at the bridge? And are these repairs hiring? I like to learn what a job involves before discovering it waist-deep.
Attempt: Arlen remains beside his companions and gestures toward the notice, asking the gray-coated person about the contact and the work.

GM: The notice poster smooths a curling corner of the paper. “Ask for Sella at the bridge. She handles the repair enquiries and takes names for work.”

At Arlen’s mention of water, the poster gives a brief nod. “They’re looking for hands to carry stone and clear debris. That’s what I was told to advertise. Sella can tell you which tasks are on dry ground and what each pays. I only agreed to put up the notices.”

The poster remains beside the board, leaving room for another question.

### Turn 2 — Elara Voss

> Thank you. One more question: do you know a man named Dain? His brother hasn't heard from him in a month. We're trying to find out whether he needs help.
Attempt: Elara stays beside her companions and asks the notice poster about Dain, keeping her hands relaxed at her sides.

GM: The notice poster turns from the board to Elara. “I don't know him by that name. I'm sorry—you've more reason to ask than someone looking for a day's wages.”

They glance toward the bridge. “Ask Sella whether he worked on the repairs. I can't say whether she knows him, but she handles the work enquiries. I wouldn't want to send you off thinking I'd seen him.”

The poster remains beside the notice. The bridge is still visible through the drizzle.

### Turn 3 — Torren Bright

> Thank you for being precise. Let's ask Sella whether Dain worked there, and what records might establish when. One question at a time.
Attempt: Torren nods to the notice poster, then gestures toward the bridge and offers to walk there with his companions, keeping his staff ready for the muddy footing.

GM: “You're welcome,” the notice poster replies, stepping aside to leave the lane clear.

The short approach to the bridge is muddy but passable, with no barrier across it. Torren's invitation leaves the three companions together beside the notice, with the way onward open.
